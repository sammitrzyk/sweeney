About Sweeney Signs

Built on Years of Hands-On Experience

Sweeney Signs is built on years of hands-on work in automotive pinstriping, hand-painted lettering, custom graphics, and commercial vinyl. The focus has always been simple: careful work, clean execution, and a finished vehicle that looks right when it leaves the shop.

Experience

Craftsmanship That Carries Across Every Project

The work began with traditional hand-painted pinstriping and vehicle lettering — brush in hand, laying out a design directly on the panel. That background carries into everything done today, including vinyl graphics, commercial wraps, and fleet work.

A lot of that work now comes from partnering with other businesses, handling paint, lettering, or vinyl installation for shops that need an experienced hand. The standard stays the same either way: clean execution, and a vehicle that looks right when it leaves the shop.
